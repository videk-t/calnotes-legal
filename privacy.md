# Cal Notes — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 11 August 2026**

Cal Notes ("the app") is a calorie and macro tracker. This policy explains what
it collects, why, and what control you have.

---

## Who we are

Cal Notes is operated by ViiP (Ivan Petrović), based in Croatia.
Contact: **vipbuildapps@gmail.com**

## What we collect

**Account information**
- Your email address, used to sign in and to recover your account.
- If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and account
  identifier from Google. We do not receive your Google password.

**Profile information you enter**
- Name (optional), age, weight, height, biological sex and activity level.
- These are used solely to calculate a suggested daily calorie goal, and to
  show your weight trend. We do not use them for anything else.

**Food and health data you create**
- The text you type describing food, the resulting calorie and macro estimates,
  the date each entry belongs to, and any weigh-ins you record.
- Photos of food you choose to take within the app.

**Technical data**
- Basic error logs from our server functions, used to diagnose failures. These
  do not contain your food entries or photos.

We do **not** collect your location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or
browsing activity. We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs.

## How your data is used

- **To provide the app**: storing your entries and showing your totals, history
  and trends.
- **To estimate nutrition**: the text you type, or the photo you take, is sent
  to Anthropic's Claude API, which returns a nutrition estimate. This is the
  core function of the app and cannot be disabled while using those features.
- **To manage your subscription**: purchase status is processed by RevenueCat
  and Google Play.

We do not sell your data. We do not share it for advertising. We do not use
your data to train AI models.

## Who processes your data

| Processor | What they receive | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Supabase** | Account, profile, entries, photos | Database, authentication and file storage |
| **Anthropic** | Food text or food photo you submit | Generating the nutrition estimate |
| **Open Food Facts** | Barcode numbers you scan | Looking up product nutrition |
| **RevenueCat** | Purchase and subscription events | Managing subscription status |
| **Google Play** | Purchase and billing information | Processing payment |

Anthropic does not use data submitted through its API to train its models.
Open Food Facts receives only the barcode number — never your account or any
other information about you.

## Where data is stored

Your data is stored on Supabase infrastructure in the region selected for the
project. Some processors listed above operate in other countries, including the
United States, so your data may be transferred outside your own country.

## How long we keep it

Your data is kept until you delete it. You can delete individual entries at any
time. Deleting your account removes everything, permanently.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export
or delete your personal data, and to object to certain processing.

**Deleting your account and data:**
1. Open Cal Notes
2. Go to **Settings → Account → Delete account**
3. Type `DELETE` to confirm

This immediately and permanently removes your account, every day and entry you
have logged, all of your food photos, your weigh-ins, and your profile. It
cannot be undone and none of it can be recovered.

**Important:** deleting your account does **not** cancel a paid subscription.
Subscriptions are billed by Google Play and must be cancelled separately in the
Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions.

If you cannot access the app, email **vipbuildapps@gmail.com** from the address
associated with your account and we will delete it for you.

## Children

Cal Notes is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your
country), and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a
child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

## Nutrition estimates are estimates

Calorie and macro figures are generated by an AI model or drawn from a public
product database. They are approximations and may be wrong. Cal Notes is not a
medical device and does not provide medical, clinical or dietary advice. Do not
rely on it to manage a medical condition — talk to a qualified professional.

## Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections. Database access is restricted
by row-level security so one account cannot read another's data, and food
photos are stored in a private bucket reachable only through short-lived signed
links. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top and, where
appropriate, notify you in the app.
