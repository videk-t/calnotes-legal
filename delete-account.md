# Delete your Cal Notes account

Cal Notes (package `com.calnotes.app`) lets you delete your account and all of
your data at any time. There are two ways to do it.

## In the app

1. Open Cal Notes and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings → Account → Delete account**.
3. Type `DELETE` to confirm.

Deletion happens immediately.

## By email

If you cannot sign in, email **vipbuildapps@gmail.com** from the address
associated with your account and ask for it to be deleted. We will confirm the
request and delete the account within 30 days.

---

## Deleting some data, without deleting your account

You do not have to close your account to remove data from it.

- **A single food entry** — open the day on **Today** or **History**, tap the
  entry to expand it, then tap **Delete**. The day's totals update immediately.
- **A food photo** — deleting the entry it belongs to removes the photo from
  our storage with it.

These deletions are immediate and permanent, and neither affects the rest of
your account.

For anything else — a weigh-in, or your profile details — email
**vipbuildapps@gmail.com** from the address on your account, telling us what to
remove. We will action it within 30 days.

## What gets deleted

Everything, permanently and immediately:

- Your account and email address
- Your profile — name, age, weight, height, biological sex, activity level
- Every day and food entry you have logged, with all calorie and macro figures
- Every food photo you have taken
- All of your weigh-ins
- Your local copy on the device, once you sign out

## What is kept, and for how long

- **Subscription records** — the purchase and renewal events tied to your
  billing account are retained while required for tax and accounting purposes
  (up to 11 years under Croatian law). These are held by Google Play and
  RevenueCat and are not linked to your food or health data after deletion.
- **Server error logs** — retained for up to 30 days for diagnostics. They
  contain no food entries, no photos, and no profile information.

Nothing else is retained, and none of the deleted data can be recovered.

## Your subscription is separate

Deleting your account does **not** cancel a paid subscription. Subscriptions
are billed by Google Play and must be cancelled there:

**Google Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions →
Cal Notes → Cancel subscription**

Cancel before deleting your account, or you will continue to be charged.
